# Owner Acceptance Test — Phase 2 (Sales)

For Jim. One step at a time. Words in **BOLD CAPITALS** are the exact button names on screen.
If anything does not match what is written here, stop and press **GET SUPPORT** — then tell Claude which step number.

Before you start: your developer (or Claude) gives you the BrittVideo address and confirms it is the **TEST** copy
(a yellow "TEST — test data only" label shows at the top). Nothing you do in the test copy charges real money or
contacts real clients.

### Part A — Mac: sign in and add a prospect
1. Open the BrittVideo address in Safari on your Mac.
2. First time only: fill in your name, email and a password (12+ characters), press **CREATE MY SUPER USER ACCOUNT**.
   Later times: fill in email and password, press **SIGN IN**.
3. You should see **Command Center** and a green pill at the top: **Work Saved & Protected**.
4. Press **+ NEW PROSPECT**.
5. Type a test business name (for example *Test Senior Living*), a website, and choose **Senior Care**.
6. Type something in **Private notes** (for example *Budget about $600*).
7. Wait two seconds. Under the green button you should see **✓ Saved**.
8. Press **ADD PROSPECT**. You should see "*… was added.*"
9. Press **ADD PROSPECT** again with the same name and website. You should see "*already in your list — no duplicate was added.*"

### Part B — iPad: in-person demo and sign-up
10. On the iPad, sign in at the same address. Open **Prospects**.
11. Next to your test business, press **IPAD DEMO**.
12. The screen changes to "*Test Senior Living + BrittVideo*". Check: **no** private notes, **no** client list, **no** menu.
13. Scroll down. You should see **Standard — $597 one-time** and **Premier — $997 + $149/month**.
14. Press **BECOME A CLIENT — PREMIER**.
15. Business name and website are already filled in. Type a name and email, read the agreement, type the name again
    under "Type your full name to sign", tick the box, press **CONTINUE TO PAYMENT**.
16. Press **SIMULATE A DECLINED CARD**. You should see "*The card was declined…*".
17. Press **PAY $997 WITH TEST CARD**. You should see **Welcome to BrittVideo!**
18. At the bottom left, press **Owner: exit demo**. You should see **BrittVideo is locked**.
19. Enter your password, press **UNLOCK**.

### Part C — Mac: the new client is ready
20. On the Mac, open **Command Center** (refresh the page).
21. Under **Needs attention today** you should see your test business: *New Client — Ready to Start · Premier*.
22. Press **OPEN PROJECT**. You should see four videos: Website (60 sec), Social A and Social B (each Landscape 16:9 ·
    Vertical 9:16 · Square 1:1) and Email Video — and **nothing** marked approved yet.
23. Press **OPEN CLIENT**. Under **Orders** you should see $997 and $149/mo, and under **Premier**: Active.

### Part D — Unsubscribe protection
24. On the client page press **CHANGE MARKETING PREFERENCE**, choose **Unsubscribed**, press **SAVE PREFERENCE**.
25. Press **CHANGE MARKETING PREFERENCE** again and choose **Active**. A new box appears asking how the client asked to
    be resubscribed. Press **CLOSE** without filling it in — the client stays Unsubscribed.

### Part E — Bring in your V2 work
26. On the computer where you use V2, open **BrittVideo V2.11.23** and press **EXPORT ALL DATA FOR UPGRADE**.
27. In BrittVideo 3.0, open **Settings → Import from V2**, press **CHOOSE EXPORT FILE**, pick the file.
28. Read the summary. Open **Projects** and check that your saved projects are there with the right approvals.

### Part F — Support
29. Press **GET SUPPORT**, type "Acceptance test", press **PREPARE SUPPORT REPORT**, then **SEND FOR SUPPORT**.

When all 29 steps match, Phase 2 is accepted. Tell Claude "Phase 2 accepted" (or the step numbers that didn't match).
